Radar privacy policy

Last updated November 1, 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. Radar has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We don't collect, store, sell or share personal information.

What stays on your device

The stocks you save, whether you've seen the welcome screen, and a copy of the latest data (so the app works offline) are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Network requests

The app downloads a public data file of government records. Like any website, the server that hosts that file (GitHub Pages) may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses, under its own privacy policy. Opening an official record takes you to that government website, which has its own policy.

Children

Radar is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change.
